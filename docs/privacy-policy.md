# Privacy Policy — Vampire Maxxing

**Effective date:** April 21, 2026
**App:** Vampire Maxxing (package `quest.kenny.vampiremaxxing`)
**Developer:** Kenny (independent developer)
**Contact:** kennydsf91@gmail.com

---

This privacy policy describes how Vampire Maxxing ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information when you install, open, and play the app on an Android device.

Vampire Maxxing is a single-player offline-first idle game. We do not operate a server for the app. We do not collect your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account.

---

## 1. What we collect on our side — nothing

We, the developer, do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from your device to our servers. The app has no backend. Your entire save state lives locally on your device in Android Preferences storage.

## 2. Data stored locally on your device

The following data is stored on your device only, never sent anywhere:

- Your progress (blood, dread, thralls owned, ascensions, achievements unlocked)
- Your settings (music on/off, haptics on/off)
- Timestamps used to compute offline progress

This data is deleted when you uninstall the app or tap **Wipe Progress** in the in-game menu.

## 3. Third-party services we use

### Google AdMob (rewarded ads)

The app displays **rewarded video ads** provided by Google AdMob. These ads are always optional — you choose to watch them in exchange for in-game rewards (boosts, offline bonuses, doubled dread on ascension). We never show forced or interstitial ads.

Google AdMob may collect:

- Device identifiers (advertising ID)
- IP address
- Approximate location (derived from IP)
- App usage data relevant to ad delivery
- Device information (model, OS version)

This collection is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or opt out of your advertising ID at any time via your Android device's **Settings → Google → Ads**.

In the European Union and European Economic Area, the app presents a Google UMP consent flow on first launch allowing you to choose between personalized and non-personalized ads.

### Capacitor runtime

The app is built with Capacitor (by Ionic). The Capacitor runtime itself does not collect any user data.

## 4. Permissions the app requests

- **Internet access** — required only for rewarded ad delivery. The app runs fully offline otherwise.

The app does NOT request access to:
- Camera
- Microphone
- Contacts
- Location
- Storage (beyond its own internal preferences)
- Phone number
- SMS

## 5. Children's privacy

Vampire Maxxing is rated **PEGI 12** / Teen. It contains stylized gothic imagery (vampires, blood) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will advise.

## 6. Data safety — summary for Play Store

Per Google Play's Data Safety requirements:

- **Data collected by the developer**: none.
- **Data shared with third parties** (via Google AdMob, with your consent):
  - Advertising ID
  - IP address
  - App activity relevant to ads
  Purpose: advertising and analytics. You may opt out via your device settings or the in-app UMP consent form.
- **Data encrypted in transit**: yes (HTTPS for ad requests).
- **Users can request data deletion**: yes — uninstall the app or use **Wipe Progress** in the in-game menu.

## 7. Your rights

You can at any time:

- Stop using the app and uninstall it.
- Wipe your local progress via the in-game **Settings → Wipe Progress** menu.
- Reset your Google advertising ID in Android settings.
- Contact us at kennydsf91@gmail.com to ask questions about this policy.

Residents of the European Union, United Kingdom, and California have additional rights under their respective privacy laws (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA). Because we do not collect personal data on our side, there is nothing for us to delete or disclose. For AdMob data, please refer to Google's privacy policy linked above.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Substantial changes will be announced in the app's release notes.
