# Portraits Prompts — 8 formes vampires

## Prompt de base à réutiliser

Colle ce bloc à chaque génération, et change uniquement la description `[CHARACTER]` :

```
Portrait illustration of a vampire character, head and shoulders, 
facing camera slightly 3/4 view. 

Style: gothic art nouveau engraving meets dark romantic oil painting, 
reminiscent of Alphonse Mucha crossed with Aubrey Beardsley, with 
subtle painterly shading. 

Color palette: deep blacks (#08050a), wine red blood tones (#a51423), 
muted antique gold (#c9a962), cold bone white skin (#f3ead8). 

Background: pure deep black void with very subtle dark purple smoke/mist, 
no other elements. No visible frame (the frame will be added in the UI).

Square 1:1 format, 1024×1024 resolution. 

Highly detailed, elegant, atmospheric, premium quality. 

NOT cartoon, NOT anime, NOT cute, NOT chibi, NOT blurry, NOT AI-generic. 
Render this as if it were a commissioned oil painting for a premium 
gothic novel cover.

CHARACTER: [REMPLACE ICI]
```

## Les 8 stades

### 1. NEWBORN (prestige 0)

```
CHARACTER: A freshly turned young vampire, pale and disoriented, 
looking slightly afraid with wide eyes. Simple ragged dark clothing, 
perhaps a plain black shirt torn at the collar. Eyes glowing with 
a very faint red light, barely visible. Small fangs just barely 
showing between slightly parted lips. Messy dark hair falling 
over forehead. The character looks almost entirely human, with 
only hints of what they will become. Vulnerable, newly awakened, 
confused. Dark ambiance but the character is the lit focal point.
```

### 2. ELDER (prestige 1-2)

```
CHARACTER: A vampire of one century, composed and confident. Pale 
elegant face with sharp cheekbones and high forehead. Wearing a 
black high-collared shirt with a subtle blood-red silk ascot or 
cravat. Eyes crimson red with a calm focused gaze. Visible fangs 
when very slightly smirking. Shoulder-length dark hair, well-groomed 
and slightly wavy. Posture confident. Hint of a dark coat or jacket 
beginning at shoulders. Aristocratic but contained.
```

### 3. LORD OF NIGHT (prestige 3-6)

```
CHARACTER: A refined vampire aristocrat, exuding an aura of authority 
and danger. Pale skin, sharply carved features, pronounced fangs 
visible in a slight smirk. A single drop of blood at the corner of 
the mouth, subtle but visible. Wearing an ornate dark coat with 
high collar and antique gold embroidery along the lapels, a dark 
crimson rose or ruby brooch at the chest. Long black wavy hair 
swept back and falling past shoulders. Eyes glowing deep red with 
slight luminosity. A small antique gold earring with a ruby drop. 
A subtle circlet or crown of blackened thorns on the brow. 
Background mist tinged with dark purple. This character is beautiful, 
dangerous, centuries old and confident in his power.
```

### 4. METHUSELAH (prestige 7-14)

```
CHARACTER: An ancient millennium-old vampire, with an unsettling 
beauty and visible wrongness. Skin paper-white with faint blue 
veins visible at temples and neck. Eyes pure blood red, no whites 
visible, glowing from within. Mouth half-parted showing longer 
pronounced fangs. Elaborate black gothic robes with heavy ornate 
antique gold details, embroidered sigils, possibly a high archaic 
collar. Hair impossibly long, silver-white streaked with black, 
flowing or floating as if suspended. Behind the head and shoulders, 
subtle translucent spectral horns or antlers beginning to emerge 
from the darkness. The face is slightly too symmetrical, too 
perfect, almost mask-like. Ancient jewelry, a heavy ornate 
necklace with a red gem. This being has seen empires rise and fall.
```

### 5. PROGENITOR (prestige 15-29)

```
CHARACTER: A primordial vampire, a bloodline founder, monstrous 
yet regal. Skin like carved marble, with hollow darkened eye 
sockets containing burning red lights deep inside. Mouth open 
revealing too many teeth, multiple rows of fangs. Wearing heavy 
ornate black robes encrusted with skull motifs, dried blood-tinted 
gold ornaments, chain of bones across the chest. A crown of bone 
and gold on the head, irregular and asymmetric. Bat-like shadow 
wings half-emerging from shoulders, dissolving into the darkness. 
Long ragged black hair with streaks of dried blood. The face is 
terrifying yet majestic, ancient beyond comprehension, a being 
of pure predatory will. Purple mist curls around the silhouette.
```

### 6. TERA OVERLORD VAMPIRE (prestige 30-49)

```
CHARACTER: An apex predator vampire god, a massive imposing figure. 
Face half-human half-nightmare: left side is a refined pale vampire 
aristocrat with a red eye, right side is monstrous with exposed 
bone and fangs, empty void-red glowing socket. Wearing impossibly 
ornate dark armor fused with living flesh and gold filigree, 
ancient sigils carved into it, subtle pulsing red veins visible in 
the armor. Crown of twisted horns and bones rising from the head. 
Behind him, a cosmic halo of swirling blood-red and darkness with 
hints of stars. Eldritch floating ornaments (small skulls, runes, 
golden symbols) hover around his head. Hair is long flowing 
darkness with occasional strands of gold. Terrifying, majestic, 
reality-bending. This is the apex form.
```

### 7. HORROR INCARNATE (prestige 50-99)

```
CHARACTER: A vampire that has transcended physical form. Barely 
humanoid silhouette of a regal crowned figure, but the body is 
composed of shifting darkness, coagulated blood, and cosmic void. 
Where the face should be: a constellation of multiple red glowing 
eyes and fangs arranged in unsettling geometric patterns (like 
the faces of angels in old religious paintings). Only hints of 
the gold ornaments remain, floating disconnected in the darkness 
around the silhouette. No clear edges - the form dissolves at 
the extremities into swirling void. The concept of "vampire" 
pushed to cosmic horror. Eldritch, unknowable, terrifying. 
Incredibly atmospheric, very dark with bright red and gold accents.
```

### 8. THE THIRST (prestige 100+)

```
CHARACTER: A vampire who has become a concept, an abstraction of 
hunger itself. No body visible, just a silhouette of a crowned 
regal figure made entirely of swirling blood, gold filigree 
threads, and deep cosmic void. The outline suggests a vampire 
king standing majestic, but details dissolve at the edges into 
pure darkness and stars. Eyes are two distant red stars far inside 
the dark void where the face would be. A single golden crown 
floats above where the head should be. The figure is surrounded 
by a subtle constellation of red dots (distant stars, or eyes). 
Unknowable, eternal, the embodiment of eternal hunger. Minimalist 
yet extremely detailed in its cosmic complexity. Feels religious.
```

## Tips finaux

- Génère **dans l'ordre** (NEWBORN → THIRST). La progression visuelle doit être ressentie.
- **Sauvegarde les résultats intermédiaires** au fur et à mesure au cas où tu veuilles en regénérer un plus tard.
- **Entre chaque portrait**, dis à ChatGPT : *"Remember the style of the previous image — same palette, same technique, same cadrage. Only the character evolves."*
- **Cadrage critique** : épaules + tête, légèrement 3/4. Ne JAMAIS prendre une vue de face totale (rigide) ni un profil pur (illisible en petit).
- **Le fond doit rester sombre et simple** — pas de décor de château, pas d'éléments narratifs. Le frame SVG du jeu donnera le décor.
