# Thralls Prompts — 8 illustrations de sbires

## Prompt de base

```
Gothic engraving illustration of [CREATURE] for a dark vampire 
game. 

Style: vintage 18th century engraving meets dark fantasy 
illustration, inspired by Gustave Doré and Edward Gorey. Fine 
line detail, cross-hatching, ornamental framing suggested but 
not drawn (the frame will be added in UI).

Color palette: deep blacks, aged bone white, muted antique gold 
accents, hints of blood red, purple mist.

Background: transparent or deep black void. No visible frame.

Centered composition, square 1:1 format, 512×512 resolution.

Highly detailed, atmospheric, premium quality, intentional and 
crafted. 

NOT cartoon, NOT anime, NOT cute, NOT simplified, NOT flat vector. 
This should look like an illustration from an ancient occult 
grimoire, reproduced beautifully for a premium mobile game.

SUBJECT: [REMPLACE ICI]
```

## Les 8 thralls

### 1. STRAY RAT

```
SUBJECT: A single detailed black rat, standing in profile on an 
old stone surface, looking sideways with a glint of red in its 
eye. Fur details precise, tail curled elegantly. One small drop 
of blood at the corner of its mouth. The rat is majestic despite 
being a rat - carries itself like a minor noble. Subtle shadow 
beneath. Gothic, not cute.
```

### 2. FERAL GHOUL

```
SUBJECT: A human skull with rotting flesh still clinging to parts 
of it, wrapped in a dark tattered hood. Empty eye sockets with a 
faint red glow deep inside. Exposed fangs in the jaw. Shown from 
the neck/chest up, with a suggestion of a skeletal emaciated body 
below. Decayed but dignified, ancient, servile to a master. Grim 
elegance.
```

### 3. FLEDGLING

```
SUBJECT: A young pale vampire, androgynous, with long dark hair 
partially covering one eye. Delicate features, small visible 
fangs, eyes glowing faintly red. Wearing a simple dark high-collared 
shirt or robe. Looks new, inexperienced but aristocratic in potential. 
Shown from chest up, slight 3/4 angle. Melancholic beauty.
```

### 4. THRALL

```
SUBJECT: A human figure in a deep hooded robe, face hidden in 
shadow except for two glowing pinpoint red eyes inside the hood. 
Hands crossed over chest in a submissive posture. Dark robes with 
subtle gold trim suggesting they serve a master. The figure 
embodies mental enslavement, servitude, quiet devotion. Shown 
from chest up, mysterious.
```

### 5. NIGHTBLADE

```
SUBJECT: A vampire assassin in profile, face mostly shadowed 
except for pale skin and a red glowing eye. Dark hood pulled up. 
Holding a long elegant curved dagger with ornate gold handle 
near the shoulder. Minimal facial detail visible. The pose 
suggests readiness, predation, silence. Dark cloak covers most 
of the body. Silhouette dramatic and dangerous.
```

### 6. BLOOD COURTESAN

```
SUBJECT: An elegant vampire figure in Victorian or Baroque court 
dress, shown from chest up. Pale beautiful face with sly half-smile 
showing small fangs. Eyes crimson with heavy dark lashes. Wearing 
an ornate high-collared dress or coat with an elaborate white 
ruffled jabot/collar at the throat, accented with a blood-red 
ribbon and a small golden pendant. Hair styled elaborately, 
perhaps with a black flower or ornament. Seductive, dangerous, 
aristocratic. Gender ambiguous or feminine.
```

### 7. ELDER

```
SUBJECT: An ancient crowned vampire figure, shown from chest up. 
Pale lined face with commanding presence, eyes blood-red and 
piercing. Long silver-streaked dark hair. Wearing a heavy dark 
robe with ornate antique gold embroidery and a thick chain around 
the neck. Small ornate crown of tarnished gold and dark gems on 
the brow. The face shows wisdom but also merciless authority. 
Slight 3/4 angle, regal bearing. This is a vampire who has ruled 
for centuries.
```

### 8. CARDINAL OF THE NIGHT

```
SUBJECT: A terrifying high-ranking vampire entity with dark curled 
horns rising from the head. Pale severe face with glowing red eyes 
and exposed long fangs in a slight predatory grin. Wearing very 
ornate dark ecclesiastical robes with heavy gold embroidery, red 
accents, and mysterious sigils. Hood or high collar framing the 
horned head. The figure is majestic and monstrous, like a dark 
pope or high priest of some forbidden faith. Shown from chest up, 
dramatic and powerful. Purple mist curls around the shoulders.
```

## Tips

- **Cadrage différent du portrait principal** : ici les sbires doivent être reconnaissables en petit (dans un cercle de ~48px dans l'UI). Privilégier la reconnaissance silhouette > détails fins.
- **Fond transparent idéal** si ChatGPT le permet, sinon fond noir profond uniforme qui se fondra dans le thème.
- **Garder le style engraving** pour les 8. Contrast fort, pas de douceurs inutiles.
- **Entre chaque génération**, dis à ChatGPT : *"Same gothic engraving style as the previous thrall image — same line quality, same palette, same level of detail. Only the subject changes."*

## Checklist post-génération

- [ ] Les 8 thralls ont le même style d'illustration (gravure)
- [ ] Palette cohérente (pas un thrall coloré en bleu alors que les autres sont rouge-or)
- [ ] Reconnaissable en petit (zoomer à 10% pour vérifier)
- [ ] Cadrage centré, symbole ou personnage clairement identifiable
- [ ] Fond sombre uniforme (ou transparent)
