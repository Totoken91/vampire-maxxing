# UI Mockup Prompt

Ce prompt a été utilisé pour générer `design/mockup.png` — le gold standard visuel.

Si tu veux regénérer ou varier le mockup, voici le prompt exact :

---

```
Create a mobile game UI mockup for a dark gothic idle game called 
"VAMPIRE MAXXING". Vertical phone screen aspect ratio (9:16, 
1080x1920 resolution).

=== OVERALL STYLE ===

Art direction: semi-ironic but deeply stylish gothic. Dark romantic 
meets art nouveau engraving meets modern premium mobile UI. Think 
Alphonse Mucha crossed with Aubrey Beardsley crossed with a 
contemporary luxury watch app interface. Absolutely NOT cartoon, 
NOT anime, NOT retro pixel art. High-end, refined, atmospheric.

Mood: a forbidden grimoire rendered as a luxurious iPhone app.

=== COLOR PALETTE (strict) ===

- Background: very deep black #08050a with subtle warm tint
- Secondary background: blood-tinted near-black #1a0f14
- Ink/text: warm off-white bone #f3ead8
- Dim text: muted warm grey #a89986
- Blood accent: rich deep red #a51423 (main numbers, accents)
- Blood glow: bright red #ff3342 (for highlights, glowing effects)
- Gold antique: muted oxidized gold #c9a962 (ornaments, frames)
- Purple shadow: mystical purple #6b2d8f (used sparingly for meta 
  elements)

80% of the screen should be deep black. Accents are SPARSE 
and deliberate. No bright fluorescent colors. No gradients like 
modern "Instagram" aesthetics — everything should feel carved, 
engraved, ancient.

=== TYPOGRAPHY ===

Three fonts visible:
1. "VAMPIRE MAXXING" title: UnifrakturCook blackletter gothic 
   (or similar ornamental gothic blackletter), in blood red 
   with subtle red glow
2. Titles and labels ("a Lord of Night", "blood"): Cormorant 
   Garamond serif, sometimes italic, elegant
3. Numbers and UI data: JetBrains Mono monospace, clean, modern 
   contrast with the serif

The contrast between ornamental gothic script + elegant serif + 
modern mono is intentional and critical to the aesthetic. 
Old bloodline + new metrics.

=== LAYOUT (top to bottom) ===

1. STATUS BAR (very top, tiny): mobile status bar with time 
   "21:34", signal/battery, subtle.

2. HEADER SECTION (compact, 3 columns):
   - Left column: "Vampire Maxxing" in ornamental gothic blackletter, 
     blood red color, glowing faintly, stacked on 2 lines
   - Center column: vertical stack of 3 lines:
     * tiny mono label "YOU ARE"
     * elegant italic serif: "a Lord of Night" in antique gold, 
       with the word "Lord" in italic blood red to emphasize rank
     * thin divider
   - Right column: 
     * tiny purple label "DREAD"
     * mono number "× 12" in mystical purple

3. ORNATE DIVIDER: a horizontal line with an elegant gothic 
   centerpiece ornament in the middle (a small trinity knot, 
   fleur-de-lis, or heart-shaped filigree in antique gold), 
   thin gold lines fading on both sides.

4. CENTRAL PORTRAIT (the hero element, takes up ~35% of screen):
   A square gothic frame with intricate antique gold filigree 
   corners and ornate border. Inside: a portrait illustration 
   of a vampire character — a refined male or ambiguous aristocrat, 
   pale skin, sharp features, visible fangs, dark ornate clothing 
   with blood-red accents, intense red glowing eyes, long dark hair, 
   3/4 view facing slightly to the side. Background inside the 
   frame: deep black void with subtle dark purple mist. The portrait 
   has a subtle red aura/glow emanating from it. 
   
   Above the portrait, a small mono text label: "— THE BLOODLINE —"
   
   Below the portrait, bottom center of the frame, in elegant 
   serif italic: "Lord of Night · Century IV"

5. BLOOD COUNTER SECTION (below portrait):
   Center-aligned, large prominent number "28,471" in JetBrains 
   Mono, in deep blood red, with subtle red glow. Above the number, 
   small italic serif label "— blood —" in faint warm grey. 
   Below the number, mono text: "+342 / per second" where "+342" 
   is in bright blood glow red, and "/ per second" is in faint warm 
   grey.

6. THY HOLLOW SERVANTS (generators list, ~30% of screen):
   Small italic serif section title centered: "— thy hollow 
   servants —" with thin gold fading lines on both sides.
   
   Below, a vertical list of 4-5 visible generator cards, each 
   card is a horizontal row:
   - Left: small ornate circular frame containing a gothic 
     illustration icon (bat, skull, ghost silhouette, hooded 
     figure, dagger)
   - Middle: generator name in small caps elegant serif 
     ("STRAY RAT", "FERAL GHOUL", "FLEDGLING", "THRALL", 
     "NIGHTBLADE"), below that in tiny mono: owned count "×47" 
     in gold and rate "23/s" in grey
   - Right: cost in mono blood red "124", below it small italic 
     serif "claim" in antique gold
   
   Each card has:
   - A dark wine-tinted background
   - 1px antique gold ornamental corner flourishes
   - Affordable cards have a subtle animated shimmer across them
   - The first 3 are affordable and visible
   - The 4th is dimmed (not affordable but unlocked)
   - The 5th is heavily dimmed with the cost shown as "— —" and 
     the label "sealed" (locked)

7. ACTION BUTTONS (bottom, 2 buttons side by side):
   Left button: gold outlined rectangular button, text "× BOOST 2×" 
   in mono caps gold, below it italic serif "15 sec" in faint gold.
   Right button: blood-red outlined rectangular button with subtle 
   glowing pulse aura, text "◈ ASCEND" in mono caps bright blood 
   red, below it italic serif "+8 dread" in faint red.
   
   Both buttons have tiny gothic corner ornaments and are 
   semi-transparent dark.

8. EDGE DECORATIONS:
   - Tiny moon icon in top-right corner, faint bone white
   - 1-2 bat silhouettes flying across the top third, tiny, 
     very faint
   - Overall subtle noise/grain texture across the entire screen 
     for atmosphere
   - Very faint red/purple fog gradients in the corners

=== IMPORTANT DETAILS ===

- Every edge of every element has some kind of ornamental detail 
  — no raw boxes. Think ex-libris plates, old book engravings, 
  antique gold leaf filigree.
- The overall feeling should be: "this looks like an expensive 
  premium app, not a cheap mobile game"
- Lots of deep shadow and negative space, NOT cluttered
- Visual hierarchy clear: portrait and blood counter are the 
  heroes, everything else supports them
- The typography contrast (gothic blackletter + elegant serif 
  + modern mono) must be very visible and deliberate
- NO bright cartoon colors, NO modern flat design, NO neon, 
  NO cutesy elements
- The ornaments should feel hand-engraved, ancient, slightly 
  worn — not perfectly crisp
- This is a PREMIUM MOBILE GAME screenshot, meant to look 
  like something a discerning design-conscious player would 
  screenshot and share

=== NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS ===

Do NOT include: cartoon characters, anime faces, bright colors, 
emoji, stock "mobile game" UI patterns (no big pink CTAs, no 
daily reward popups, no coin currency icons), no Instagram-style 
gradients, no neomorphism, no glass-morphism blur, no Material 
Design, no iOS native controls visible other than the status 
bar at the very top.

Render this as if it were a pixel-perfect app screenshot taken 
from a real released premium Android game.
```

## Variations possibles

Pour générer des screenshots additionnels (pour le Play Store listing), adapter :

### Écran "Ascend modal"
Remplacer la zone des thralls par un modal overlay avec :
- Titre "ASCEND THE BLOODLINE" en gothic blackletter rouge
- Description italique de transition ("You will become an Elder. The current run resets but your power will grow forever.")
- Bouton de confirmation
- Option "INVOKE THE CURSE · ▶" pour l'ad-watch variant

### Écran "Apothecary" (store)
- 3 cartes de skins côte à côte avec preview animé
- Titre "APOTHECARY" ornementé
- Prix 2.99€ en mono

### Écran "Ascension transition" (cinématique)
- Portrait qui se dissout en particules rouges
- Au centre, titre massif en gothic blackletter "ELDER" qui se révèle
- Flash rouge en arrière-plan
